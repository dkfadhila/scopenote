# ScopeNote article media

These screenshots show the actual ScopeNote frontend with fictional Tanah Studio data loaded through an isolated local API fixture. The example scope is hand-authored and already exists in `src/domain.js`; it is not evidence of model accuracy, a measured generation run, or customer use. No production account identifiers, emails, session tokens or private client documents appear in the screenshots.

`proposal-pdf.png` is the first page of the PDF downloaded through the app's actual PDF export. The sample uses 42 hours, US$65/hour and 15% contingency. The exact arithmetic is US$3,139.50; the application's current display rounds this to US$3,140.

Architecture, payment and privacy figures were authored as editable OpenSlide pages in `open-slide/slides/scopenote-article/index.tsx` in the authoring workspace and captured from its preview route. Their source is archived in `docs/figures/scopenote-article.tsx` for inspection.

The 45-second HyperFrames walkthrough is an edited sequence of real UI screenshots, not an uninterrupted screen recording and not a timed AI generation. Its English on-screen labels explain the flow. The existing narrated promo on DEV is separate and remains attached to the article header.
